01 Process

Four steps
to your original.

Enquiry

Tell me your idea – subject, preferred size, style and where it will hang. The more detail, the better.

Agreement

We go through the details, you get a quote and, if needed, first sketches or references.

Painting

I paint your piece by hand on canvas – with markers, spray or both. You get updates along the way.

Hand-over

The finished, signed piece is safely packed and shipped – or handed over in person if you’re in the area.

03 FAQ

Good to know.

How long does a commission take?

It depends on size and detail – usually a few weeks. You’ll get a realistic timeframe together with the quote.

Can I send my own photo as a reference?

Yes, of course. Ideally a sharp, well-lit photo. I’ll translate it into my pop art and graffiti style.

Do I get a sketch first?

If needed, yes – so we can agree on framing, colours and style before I start on the canvas.

Do you ship?

Yes, across Germany and safely packed. Around Frankfurt a personal hand-over is also possible. For international shipping, just ask.

Ready for your original?

Send me your idea – I’ll get back to you quickly.
