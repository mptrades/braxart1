01 My story

Authenticity
above all.

Pop ArtGraffitiStreet artCanvas Posca markersMontana sprayCommissionsOriginals

Hi, I’m Max – better known as Braxart Born in 2006 and raised with a sketchbook, a spray can and the urge to express myself, I started early to find my own visual language. What began as a hobby quickly became my passion – and today my career goal.

My art moves between pop art and graffiti. Clean lines, strong contrasts, bold colours and often an ironic or socially critical undertone define my work. I love playing with pop culture symbols, urban flair and personal influences. Every piece should not only look good but carry something – an emotion, an attitude or a story.

Over the past few years I’ve kept developing my style. At first it was pure experimentation: markers, spray, canvas, digital – anything that made me curious. Today I’ve found my way: a mix of street art, modern pop art elements and a personal signature that people recognise.

What matters to me: authenticity. I don’t copy role models – I get inspired, but I do my own thing. With commissions, too, my focus is on combining the client’s idea with my style to create something unique. Canvas, murals or digital prints – I stand for quality, originality and real collaboration.

Beyond art I’m into design, fashion and business. I dream big – my goal is to succeed with what I love. Not to fit some image, but because I believe art can change things: spaces, thoughts, perspectives.

Braxart isn’t just my artist name – it’s my project, my vision, my path. Glad you’re here.
